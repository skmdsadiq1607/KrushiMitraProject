# Verified Agricultural Data Sources

Every disease profile, pest threshold, and crop condition within **Crop Health AI** is curated from authoritative Indian agricultural research bodies.

---

## 1. Primary Research Institutes

### 1. Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)
- **Role**: Apex body for coordinating, guiding, and managing research and education in agriculture in India.
- **Reference**: [icar.org.in](https://icar.org.in)
- **Data Extracted**: National disease advisory thresholds, integrated pest management (IPM) standards, and biosecurity protocols.

### 2. Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU) Agritech Portal
- **Role**: Leading state agricultural university providing comprehensive field-level crop protection guides.
- **Reference**: [agritech.tnau.ac.in](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in)
- **Data Extracted**: Symptom descriptions, favorable epidemic conditions, cultural and bio-agent management.

### 3. ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru
- **Role**: Premier national institute for horticultural crops (Tomato, Chilli, Brinjal, Onion).
- **Reference**: [iihr.res.in](https://iihr.res.in)
- **Data Extracted**: Tomato Early/Late Blight and Leaf Curl virus epidemiology; Chilli anthracnose die-back management.

### 4. ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur
- **Role**: Specialized research on cotton fiber crops.
- **Reference**: [cicr.icar.gov.in](https://cicr.icar.gov.in)
- **Data Extracted**: Bollworm complex management, Bacterial Blight (Angular leaf spot), ETL thresholds.

### 5. ICAR-Indian Institute of Rice Research (IIRR), Hyderabad
- **Role**: Specialized grain and paddy research institute.
- **Reference**: [icar-iirr.org](https://icar-iirr.org)
- **Data Extracted**: Rice blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*), Sheath blight, and stem borer IPM protocols.

### 6. ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), Ludhiana
- **Role**: Apex research body for maize and coarse cereals.
- **Reference**: [iimr.icar.gov.in](https://iimr.icar.gov.in)
- **Data Extracted**: Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*) monitoring and biopesticide recommendations.

### 7. ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore
- **Role**: National institute for oilseed soybean pathology.
- **Reference**: [iisrindore.icar.gov.in](https://iisrindore.icar.gov.in)
- **Data Extracted**: Soybean rust (*Phakopsora pachyrhizi*) and Yellow Mosaic Virus vector control.
